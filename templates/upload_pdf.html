How it works
  1. Upload any vendor PDF invoice.
  2. Text is extracted automatically.
  3. An AI model parses the text into structured fields.
  4. You review and correct the extracted data on the next screen.
  5. Click Import — data goes straight into PostgreSQL.

Tip: Set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY for Claude (cloud, best accuracy), or point OLLAMA_URL to a local or remote Ollama server. Without either, you can still upload a PDF and fill the form manually.

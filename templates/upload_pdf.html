📄 Upload PDF Invoice

Upload a vendor PDF invoice. The app will extract its text and use an AI model to pre-fill the data. Priority: Anthropic Claude (cloud) → Ollama (local or remote). Either way, you will review and edit everything on the next screen before importing.

Back to list
📋 How it works
  1. Upload any vendor PDF invoice.
  2. Text is extracted automatically (scanned PDFs included via OCR).
  3. An AI model parses the text into structured fields.
  4. You review and correct the extracted data on the next screen.
  5. Click Import — data goes straight into PostgreSQL.

Tip: Set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY for Claude (cloud, best accuracy), or point OLLAMA_URL to a local or remote Ollama server. Without either, you can still upload a PDF and manually fill the form.
